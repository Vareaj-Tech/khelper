6

Booking — the transaction layer

Directory from free government open data (HIRA). v1 booking is concierge — K'Helper relays to partner clinics via KakaoTalk. The moat: every booking arrives with the patient's intake already translated into Korean. Clinics get foreign customers; patients get care without fear.

Why this focus: verified research (July 2026) shows healthcare access is the sharpest pain for Korea's 2.84M foreigners — 400–600K uninsured, and under 10% hospital interpreter coverage for Khmer and Bengali speakers. v1 tells one story: "Get healthcare and insurance help in your language." Everything on these five screens serves that sentence. CV Builder and Translator Marketplace are built and hidden — they return as Phase 2 retention features once the wedge lands.
